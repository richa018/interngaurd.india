Internship red flags to watch for

💸

Asks you to pay

Real internships never charge registration, training, kit or "security" fees.

📧

Free email address

Offer letters from gmail/yahoo instead of a company domain are a warning sign.

⚡

Instant selection

Selected without an interview or any test? Be careful.

📱

Only WhatsApp / Telegram

Refuses calls, video meets or an office address? Walk away.

📄

No offer letter

No written terms, stipend or duration before you start.

🔍

Cannot be found online

No website, LinkedIn page or reviews. Check here first.

Frequently asked questions

Is InternVerify free?

Yes. Searching, reading reports and submitting a report are free.

Where does the data come from?

Every record is stored in our MongoDB database. Companies are listed from public information and reviewed by our admin team; the Verified badge means our team checked the details.

What does the trust score mean?

A 0-100 score based on verification status and approved student reports. It is guidance, not a guarantee.

How do I report a scam?

Search the company, scroll to "Report this company" and submit. Approved reports update the score.

A company is missing. What now?

Search anyway: if it is not listed, treat that as a reason to be careful and ask for proof of registration.
